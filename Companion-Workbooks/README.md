# The Norwood Files — Companion Workbooks

Official companion workbooks for **The Norwood Files** by Dan Bleigh — a business novel about process risk, internal audit, root cause and organizational knowledge in a manufacturing plant, current to the 2026 edition of ISO 9001.

Every form printed in Part Five of the book is here as a working Excel file, with the trigger rules, the calculations and the dashboards already written into the formulas. Free, no registration. Nothing in the book depends on these files — every form is also printed in full in the book.

## The four files

| File | Companion to | Working forms |
|---|---|---|
| `Risk_Register_Workbook.xlsx` | Part One — The Register | Forms 1–5: process flow, risk register, action plan and effectiveness verification, review log, supplier risk — plus rating scales, dashboard, audit evidence map and three worked example registers |
| `Finding_Register_Workbook.xlsx` | Part Two — The Audit | Forms 6–11: audit program, audit plan, trail record, sample record, finding register, closure verification — plus dashboard and three worked audits |
| `Candidate_Register_Workbook.xlsx` | Part Three — The Investigation | Forms 12–16: investigation record, candidate register, containment record, attribute measurement study, corrective action — plus dashboard and three worked investigations |
| `Knowledge_Inventory_Workbook.xlsx` | Part Four — The Supplier and the Knowledge | Forms 17–21: supplier consequence ranking, assessment record, knowledge inventory, transfer tracker, transition gap analysis — plus dashboard and three worked assessments |

The numbered sheets carry the same Form numbers as Part Five of the book: Form 13 in the book is Form 13 in these files.

## How to use them

Open the workbook for the part you are working from, read the **START HERE** sheet, then open the worked EXAMPLE closest to your own operation before touching the blank forms. Blue text on cream is where you type; black bold is calculated — do not overwrite it. Each register recalculates its risk priority numbers, trigger flags and dashboard live as you type.

## Requirements

Standard `.xlsx` — Microsoft Excel 2013 or later, LibreOffice Calc, or any current spreadsheet application. No macros, no external links, no add-ins.
